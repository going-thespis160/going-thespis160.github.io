🚀 What Is AlphaSteg?

AlphaSteg is a privacy tool that lets you hide sensitive information inside music files. Think of it like a secret compartment in your favorite song – nobody can tell the audio has been modified, but you can store encrypted text messages, documents, or even alternate audio streams within the track. The music still plays perfectly on any device or streaming service.

Whether you want to send a confidential message, securely back up important files, or just have fun with digital stealth, AlphaSteg makes it possible with just a few clicks. No coding experience needed – it's designed for everyday users like you.

🎓 How It Works – Simple Examples

📨 Hiding a Text Message

  1. Open AlphaSteg and select "Encode a Message."
  2. Choose your music file (e.g., a song you downloaded from Bandcamp).
  3. Type your secret message in the box.
  4. Set a password – you'll need it to decode later.
  5. Click "Encode." AlphaSteg creates a new music file with your hidden message inside.
  6. Send that file to anyone – they'll just hear normal music.

🔓 Decoding a Message

  1. Open AlphaSteg and select "Decode a Message."
  2. Choose the music file that contains the hidden message.
  3. Enter the password the sender shared with you.
  4. Your hidden message appears instantly. That's it!
🔒 Security tip: Use a strong, unique password. Without it, nobody can extract your hidden data – even if they know it's there.

📖 Frequently Asked Questions

Is AlphaSteg legal?

Yes. Steganography (hiding data in files) is a legitimate technique used for privacy, secure communication, and digital rights management. Always obey local laws and respect copyright – use it on your own files.

Will my streaming service remove the hidden data?

Usually not. AlphaSteg is designed to survive standard audio processing (compression, re-encoding) used by Spotify, Tidal, Bandcamp, and others. However, extreme low-bitrate re-encoding might reduce capacity.

Can I hide any type of file?

Yes – text, PDFs, images, ZIP archives, executable installers, anything. The only limit is that your hidden file must be smaller than the audio file itself.

Is my data encrypted?

Absolutely. AlphaSteg uses AES-256 encryption – the same standard used by banks and governments – before hiding your data in the audio.

Does it work on macOS or Linux?

Currently, AlphaSteg is optimized for Windows. Mac and Linux versions are planned for the future.
